Two students walk out of their final exam. “That was a piece of cake,” one says. “Chocolate or vanilla?” the other replies.

It sounds like a simple mix up. But it’s a small window into something bigger. Every culture is full of sayings that only make sense from the inside. Learning each other’s is how we actually start to understand each other.

I believe our school is very multicultural and diverse. Students often mistake culture for difference. The cultures we all proudly carry should be something we connect through, rather than something we use to point out how different our food or languages are. This website shows you that connection. So welcome to Cultures Connect. Kai, Mentor Group 910
